Privacy

Screen Explainer Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-19. This policy covers Screen Explainer, a Windows app that runs through Anna. It does not cover the Pointframe desktop app, CLI, or MCP server; that is a separate policy, on the Pointframe privacy policy page.

How the app runs

Screen Explainer is for Windows only. It runs through Anna: its app window opens in the Anna web dashboard in your browser, and a small helper program runs on your PC through Anna's local agent.

What leaves your PC, and where it goes

Everything below is sent through Anna's service, not to a server run by Pointframe.

  • The window list. The titles (cut to 100 characters), process names, and sizes of up to 30 visible windows are sent through Anna's service to the app window in your browser, so you can pick one.
  • A preview and the recognized text. A small preview of the window you chose (about 900 pixels wide, JPEG) and the text recognized in it are sent through Anna's service to the app window, so you can see and edit them.
  • Your request to the AI model. When you press Explain, Summarize, Translate, or Action items, the text in the text box and the instruction are sent through Anna to the AI model that Anna provides. The screenshot is not sent to the model.

What stays on your PC

The window you choose is captured in memory on your PC, and its text is read with Windows OCR on your PC. The full-size image is not saved and not uploaded.

Very large results are briefly written to a temporary file on your PC, as Anna's protocol requires. Anna's agent reads and deletes that file.

What Pointframe collects

Pointframe, the developer of Screen Explainer, runs no server for this app, collects no telemetry or analytics from it, and stores nothing.

Anna and the AI model

How Anna processes and retains data, including what passes through its service and the AI model it provides, is governed by Anna's own terms and privacy policy. See anna.partners.

Be careful what you pick

Do not pick windows that show passwords, payment details, or other secrets.

This is not the Pointframe privacy policy

The Pointframe privacy policy covers the Pointframe desktop app, CLI, and MCP server, not this app.

Contact and support

Questions, requests, and privacy concerns: open an issue at github.com/dimitar-radenkov/Pointframe/issues.
